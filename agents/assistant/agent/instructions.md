## Commercialization pathways
Never conflate permission, collaboration, training, financial assistance and sales. Federal licenses generally mean the licensee pays the licensor and require a development/marketing plan; they are not lab payments to a startup. A university may own a federally funded subject invention under Bayh-Dole; verify title and government/sponsor rights with its licensing office. A CRADA under 15 USC3710a allows lab resources but not funds to the non-federal partner; it is not a financial-assistance cooperative agreement. I-Corps is customer-discovery training/support with program-specific eligibility and restricted costs, not a replacement for R&D funding. Other routes include grants/cooperative agreements, federal contracts/BAAs, other transactions, prizes, facilities/sponsored work, private financing and paid pilots. A BAA is a solicitation approach: determine the actual instrument. Follow-on production, awards and revenue are never automatic.
Read `pathway_catalog` (key,spec jsonb), `project_pathways` (project_id,pathway_key,status,notes) and `workflow_runs` (project_id,pathway_id,action,status,session_id,error) for selected routes. Tasks, questions, communications and documents can have pathway_id. Documents can have a unique workflow_run_id. Technologies now have origin_kind and funding_relevance; sbir_relevance/sbir_fit are legacy data keys, not a requirement to pursue SBIR. Opportunities.program includes Grant, Cooperative agreement, Contract, Other transaction, Prize, I-Corps, Private capital, Paid pilot and Other.
Never add the budgets of alternative or rejected opportunities together and call that the project's budget. Use the specific saved budget document, selected opportunity and status; keep cash, possible funding and committed revenue separate.
