description: Research financing and support options for founders, investors and fund managers, distinguishing instruments, applicants and intermediaries.
---
This is a research map, not a verified directory of currently open funding. Open the relevant source and then its current program-specific notice. Agency/operator starting links are not evidence of eligibility or current availability. If a source moved, search the official agency site. Never claim all programs have bespoke application automation in this app.

Start with the user’s role, stage, use of money, geography and intended activity. Ask only the next useful question in ordinary chat; for a bounded workflow, record missing facts as unknown and give conditional routes. A company seeking investment is not applying to license a fund. Repayable debt, fund leverage, investor tax benefits, insurance, guarantees, grants, contracts and advice are separate. Do not add them as equivalent available cash. Procurement preferences and licenses do not mean awards. No automatic signatures, submissions, payments or tax/legal determinations.

Never infer protected-identity, veteran or disadvantaged-business eligibility from a name or profile. Ask neutrally when directly relevant and let the user decline. Do not copy sensitive personal documents into a general draft unnecessarily.

Annual program totals, national allocations, historic matches and awards do not establish currently available funding. SBA SBIC leverage varies by structure (standard, Accrual, Reinvestor). USDA currently points to non-leveraged RBIC applications. STEP export support is different from NCI STEP. BARDA and ARPA-H belong to HHS, not DoD. Opportunity Zone rules have changed: check the applicable investment date, legislation and transition guidance rather than relying solely on an older FAQ. NMTC investor credits are not grants to operating companies. GovTribe is commercial, not an official government portal.

Audience: founder. Instrument: Debt. Access: Participating lender / CDC / nonprofit intermediary.
Repayable financing, not grant income. Uses, borrower contribution, collateral, guarantees and ability to repay are program- and lender-specific. Microloans are intermediary loans, not a direct SBA loan to the business.

Current funding/eligibility: not checked.

### SBIC (standard, Accrual and Reinvestor)
Audience: both. Instrument: Fund license / leverage. Access: Fund licensing; companies approach licensed funds.
Leverage is debt to the licensed fund, not a startup grant. SBA distinguishes standard, Accrual and Reinvestor structures; do not assume a universal 2:1 match. Verify current limits, fees, private-capital and investment rules.
Starting source: https://www.sba.gov/partners/sbics/apply-be-sbic
Current funding/eligibility: not checked.

### DFC
Audience: both. Instrument: Debt / equity / political-risk insurance. Access: Project or fund-specific DFC process.
Match the project or fund to current country, sector, development, policy and underwriting rules. India or Guyana is not blanket evidence of eligibility.
Starting source: https://www.dfc.gov/apply
Current funding/eligibility: not checked.

### RBIC
Audience: both. Instrument: Rural investment-fund license. Access: Fund application; companies approach licensed RBICs.
USDA’s current public application guidance addresses non-leveraged RBICs. Do not portray an RBIC license as an automatic SBIC-style federal capital match.
Starting source: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/rural-business-investment-program
Current funding/eligibility: not checked.

### New Markets Tax Credit (NMTC)
Audience: both. Instrument: Tax-credit investment. Access: Allocated CDE / qualifying investment and project.
The investor credit and financing reaching a qualifying business are distinct. Verify allocation access, low-income-community and project rules, structure and recapture exposure with advisers.
Starting source: https://www.cdfifund.gov/programs-training/programs/new-markets-tax-credit
Current funding/eligibility: not checked.

### Opportunity Zones
Audience: both. Instrument: Tax incentive. Access: Qualified Opportunity Fund / qualifying investment.
Investment date and statutory regime matter. Review 2025 legislation and 2026/2027 transitional guidance with a tax adviser. Do not apply old holding-period benefits to every new investment.
Starting source: https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/opportunity-zones-frequently-asked-questions
Current funding/eligibility: not checked.
